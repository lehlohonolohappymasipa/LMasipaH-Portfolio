What a profitable strategy needs

Every EA we build answers four questions. If any one is vague, the automation will magnify it.

Swipe horizontally to inspect the chart.

  1. Conditions for entry. The objective, repeatable setup that must be true.
  2. Entry position. The exact price or trigger where the order is placed.
  3. Stop loss. Where the idea is proven wrong and the loss is capped.
  4. Take profit. Where the trade is closed, sized against the risk taken.

The profitable trader triad

Strategy alone doesn’t pay. A profitable trader needs all three, and an EA covers the one that fails most often: psychology.

  • Strategy: a tested edge with clear rules.
  • Risk management: position sizing and drawdown limits that keep you in the game. Automations keeps it consistent
  • Psychology: executing the plan every time. Automation removes hesitation, revenge trades and overrides.
